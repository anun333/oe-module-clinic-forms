# Conformance

Generated by `tools/conformance.php` from `src/Paper/Conformance.php`, the rules `tests/Paper/ConformanceTest.php` enforces. Do not edit; run `php tools/conformance.php` after changing a document, and `--check` in CI.

Every shipped document is held to what it claims to follow, by the kind of its source:

- **Rule:** every element of its rule is present (`data/requirements.json`, `data/packs/us/requirements.json`).
- **Instrument:** a validated one matches LOINC's reference question by question, with its answers, points and score range (`data/instruments`). Any other carries its publisher's wording unchanged (`data/references`).
- **Written:** our own wording. Nothing outside to hold it to; patient documents are held to a reading level of grade 8 or below.

A document not cleared for public release is held, with its reason (`data/release-gates.json`): it ships as a draft, marked Draft in the picker and on every printed page, and in no packet. A document whose answers carry SNOMED CT US Extension codes is held whether listed or not.

Checked beside this, outside the module: every FHIR export against FHIR R4 and SDC 4.0.0 (the HL7 validator), skip logic and totals in OpenEMR's own questionnaire runtime, and patient PDFs against PDF/UA-1. The README says where.

**99 documents:** 44 written, 36 rule, 19 instrument. Nothing out of conformance. Held for release: none.
